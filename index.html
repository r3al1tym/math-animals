    How Claude made it

    Read, implement, draw, review.

    Claude Code, running Opus 5.5, wrote every line. I directed and gave notes.

    154Claude sessions
    92agent-hours
    9,900tool calls
    8.5 hlongest unattended run
    1. A lead session wrote the concept, one builder brief and a card per animal: its rule, its sources, its moment.
    2. Ten builder sessions worked in parallel. Each read the research on its animal, implemented the model, and drew the animal from the model’s output.
    3. 97 reviewer sessions, each new to the work, judged every round from the rendered frame alone and returned PASS or REVISE with ranked changes.
    4. Fact-checker sessions verified every number in the captions against a primary source.

    Because every image is code, the series shares one light, one palette and one line weight, and any detail can change without disturbing the rest. That control is what made the film possible.

    What didn’t work. The forms hold up, but the surfaces still read as computed. Only the dragonfly passed its reviewer, and a round aimed at photographic realism returned REVISE on all 45 reviews.
